Welcome to Surya Dental Care

Healthy, beautiful smiles built with comfort and clinical excellence.

At Surya Dental Care, we believe that a healthy, beautiful smile is the foundation of confidence and overall well-being. Located in the heart of KPHB, Hyderabad, our clinic is dedicated to providing world-class, comprehensive dental care tailored to the unique needs of every patient.

We combine state-of-the-art dental technology with a gentle, patient-first approach to ensure your visit is as comfortable, stress-free, and effective as possible. Whether you are visiting us for a routine check-up, advanced restorative procedures, or a complete smile makeover, our team is committed to delivering exceptional results with the highest standards of hygiene and clinical excellence.

The practice is led by Dr. Goli Swathi, Owner and Chief Dentist at Surya Dental Care. With a deep commitment to clinical precision and patient comfort, Dr. Goli Swathi has built a reputation for delivering personalized dental solutions in a warm and welcoming environment.
